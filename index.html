Supported living

Your own front door, your own tenancy, and support built around how you want to live. Supported living is not a care home: it's your home, with the right level of help to make it work for you.

Ask about supported living

  • Your own tenancy

    You hold your own tenancy or licence agreement, so the home is yours, not ours. You choose how it's decorated and who visits.

  • Support that flexes with you

    From a few visits a week to round-the-clock support, the hours and type of help are built around your needs and can change as your life does.

  • Shared or individual homes

    Live alone, with a partner, or share with housemates you get on with. We help you find a setting that suits you.

  • Staying connected

    Support to keep in touch with family and friends, get out into the community, and build the routine you want.

  • Moving in, made easier

    We help with finding a property, benefits and paperwork, and settling in, so the move itself feels manageable.

Support for you, not a label

Every person is different. We start by listening to who you are and what matters to you, then build support around that.

Learning disabilities

Support to make your own choices, learn everyday skills and take part in the things you enjoy. Information in the way that works for you, including easy read and pictures.

Autism

Calm, predictable support that respects how you experience the world. We plan around your routines, sensory needs and how you like to communicate.

Physical disabilities

Practical help at home and out in the community, from personal care to getting around, so you can do more on your own terms.

Other disabilities

This includes sensory impairments and long-term conditions. If you are not sure whether we are the right fit, get in touch. We will listen and help you find the right support.

What support can include

Every plan is different. Support is built around the skills and goals that matter to you, and can flex or reduce over time as you build confidence.

Ask about support

  • Cooking and meal planning
  • Money management
  • Laundry and household skills
  • Safe travel and public transport
  • Appointments and healthcare
  • Personal care
  • Online safety
  • Relationships and boundaries
  • Community participation
  • College, volunteering and employment
  • Preparing for less intensive support where appropriate

Getting started is simple

You do not need a diagnosis letter or a perfect plan. Just say hello.

  1. Say hello

    Call, email or use the form. You, a family member or a professional can get in touch.

  2. We listen

    We meet you where you feel comfortable and ask what matters to you.

  3. We plan together

    Your goals and preferences go into a plan written in a way you understand.

  4. Support begins

    Your support starts, at the pace you choose.

  5. We keep checking in

    Regular reviews make sure the plan keeps up with you.

What you can expect from us

We listen first

Every plan begins with what you want, not with what a form asks for.

You make the choices

We give you clear options and respect the decisions you make.

We communicate your way

Easy read, pictures, plain words, calls or texts. Whatever works best for you.

We keep improving with you

We review your support regularly, and it is easy to tell us when something is not right.
